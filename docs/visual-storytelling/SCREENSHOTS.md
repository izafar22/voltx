At 375px, full-page heights changed from 5,544 to 3,067px (homepage), 6,076 to 4,469px (Services), and 3,202 to 2,665px (About). How It Works grows from 3,964 to 4,126px because five substantial scenes replace small diagrams; this is purposeful journey content rather than repeated filler. Heights use the controlled screenshot font environment and are not a layout contract.

Visual review selected the corrected PCB solder-joint scene, unlabelled test instruments, blank enquiry interface, price-free consultation and protected return packaging. The original undersized assessment/repair crops were replaced after the first screenshot pass exposed pixelation. The final after captures show the revised assets and compact shared footer coverage.

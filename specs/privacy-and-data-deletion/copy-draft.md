## Privacy Policy

### About this policy

This policy explains how RushX handles information when you visit evchargerrepair.in, sign in, submit a charger repair request or use our repair coordination services.

### Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, we handle account and contact details, verified mobile numbers, email and linked Google account information, saved pickup/delivery addresses, charger details, reported faults, repair photographs and information you send our team. Repair records may include inspection findings, estimates, your decisions, status history, payment amounts and references, and courier/tracking details.

### Why we use it

We use this information to manage your account, verify sign-in, assess and coordinate repair requests, share estimates, record approvals and payment verification, arrange relevant repair logistics, provide service updates, respond to questions and protect the service from misuse.

### Sign-in and WhatsApp updates

WhatsApp verification messages help you sign in or verify a mobile number. They do not automatically enrol a repair in lifecycle updates. Repair updates are optional and selected for an individual repair using your verified number. You can stop those updates from repair details and continue following progress in your account. A message already sent or in flight may still arrive. Stopping updates does not itself delete your account or repair/payment records.

### Service providers and access

We use service providers for functions such as hosting/database operation, Google sign-in, WhatsApp delivery and repair logistics. We share relevant information only as needed to support the function being used, such as arranging an eligible collection or return. Our team accesses account and repair information through the service's access controls.

### Cookies and website technologies

The service uses browser storage and session mechanisms to keep you signed in securely and remember settings such as your theme preference. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's font service when the page loads. RushX does not intentionally run website analytics or session-recording code on these pages. Service and infrastructure logs may still contain technical information needed to operate, secure and diagnose the service.

### Retention and requests

We retain information needed to operate accounts and repair services and to address relevant support, payment, security and recordkeeping needs. Some verification/session records have expiry controls. You can ask us about access, correction or deletion of information associated with you. We review ownership and the records involved and explain any information that needs to be retained and why.

### Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact rushx779@gmail.com. We identify material updates through the page's updated date.

## Data-deletion Instructions

### Request deletion

Email rushx779@gmail.com with the subject “RushX data-deletion request”. Identify the mobile number or email associated with your account and, if relevant, the repair request number. Describe whether you want an address removed, particular information reviewed, or your account information considered for deletion.

We may ask for proportionate information to verify that the request belongs to you. Do not send your password, sign-in OTP, payment PIN or unrelated identity documents with the initial request.

### What happens next

We review the request and the records involved, then explain what can be deleted or anonymised and any information that needs to remain for an ongoing repair, payment reconciliation, dispute, security or other applicable recordkeeping need.

You can edit/remove eligible saved addresses from your profile. Whole-account deletion is currently a request to our team; this page does not perform deletion or change a repair/payment record by itself. Requests involving information processed by another service may also need that provider's process; we explain relevant limits when responding.

### Stop repair updates

To stop optional WhatsApp repair messages, open that repair's details and choose “Stop WhatsApp updates”. This is separate from requesting deletion and does not remove service records or recall messages already sent.
